# Reading articles

The reader is a web page served by the device. It works in any browser, on any
phone or computer joined to the device's network, and it needs no app.

## The library page

`192.168.4.1` lists your libraries: this guide
first, then every pack you have installed. Each library shows its article count
and opens into its articles.

## An article

Articles are plain HTML with a title, the text, and a link to the next one.
There is a **Download this article** link at the bottom: it saves the article
as an `.html` file your browser can keep, print, or share. Packs are built so
that each article is complete on its own, which is why a downloaded article
still looks right away from the device.

## Reading on several devices at once

Up to four devices can use the reader at the same time. The device serves a few
articles per second; that is plenty for reading, and it is not meant for bulk
downloading a whole pack.

## What you cannot see

The size of the library, the compression used, and the storage layout are all
the device's business. Articles always arrive as ordinary web pages: no client
needs to understand the pack format, and an old browser works as well as a new
one.
