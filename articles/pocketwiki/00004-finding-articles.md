# Finding articles

There are two ways to look for something: search, and browsing.

## Search

Type into the search box at the top of the library page, or open
`/search?q=water` directly. Search looks for your
words at the start of a title, ignoring case and extra spaces, and it searches
**every library on the device at once**: this guide and every installed pack.
It shows the first 20 matches with the library each one came from.

Matching is literal: `photosynthesis` finds "Photosynthesis" and
"Photosynthesis in leaves", but not "How plants make sugars". If a search comes
back empty, try a shorter word or the beginning of the term you remember.

## Browsing

`/browse` lists every article title on the device in
alphabetical order, a few hundred at a time, and loads more as you scroll. It
is the fastest way to see what is actually in a pack before you read it.

## Following links

Articles link to each other where the source material did, and those links stay
inside the device: a link in an installed pack points at the same pack. If a
linked article was not included in the pack, the link stays on the page but
leads nowhere. Packs are selections, not complete encyclopedias.
