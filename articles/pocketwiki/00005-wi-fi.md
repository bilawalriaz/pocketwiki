# Connecting to Wi-Fi

The device always runs its own access point so you can read. Connecting it to
your home Wi-Fi as well is optional, and only needed when you want it to
download packs by itself.

## Setting it up

1. Join the device's own network (`PocketWiki`).
2. Open `192.168.4.1/manage`.
3. Choose your network from the scan list, or type its name, and enter the
   password.
4. The device reboots its station link and joins. The address it received is
   shown on the manage page and on the OLED, if you have one.

Use a **2.4 GHz** network. The chip has no 5 GHz radio.

## One radio, two roles

The access point and the station share a single radio, so they must use the
same channel. When the device joins a network, its own access point moves onto
that network's channel, briefly interrupting readers. Everything settles within
a second or two.

Because they share air time, a weak upstream network makes the reader slower
for everyone. The device watches the signal and, if it stays weaker than about
-87 dBm, it drops the uplink and keeps serving its own network. Your saved
credentials are kept: move the device closer and set the Wi-Fi again from
`/manage`.

## Turning it off again

Clear the network name in `/manage` and save. The device goes back to being an
offline library on its own network, with nothing to interrupt it.
